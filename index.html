About

I'm a junior full stack developer based in Nigeria, focused on building web applications that hold up in practice — authentication that's actually secure, APIs that are easy to reason about, and interfaces that don't get in the user's way.

I graduated with a B.Sc. in Software Engineering from Babcock University (2021–2024), and most of what I've built since has come from taking on real CRUD applications end to end: designing the schema, wiring up the API, and getting the auth flow right before touching a single line of UI.

Outside of client work, I run ZeroX, a YouTube channel covering anime and games — which is probably why I care more than most junior devs about whether an interface actually feels good to use.
